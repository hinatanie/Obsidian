## 2. How a "Reduction Pipeline" Makes an AI Agent Affordable
Companies are building **AI Investigator Agents** (LLM-driven systems) to read logs and find root causes automatically. However, sending millions of raw logs directly into an LLM like GPT-4 or Claude is **extremely expensive** and exceeds token limits.
- **The Solution: A Reduction Pipeline.** This is a software layer that filters, deduplicates, compresses, and groups logs _before_ passing them to the AI.
- **How it works:** It strips away standard, repetitive system logs (e.g., successful HTTP `200` pings) and only surfaces structural patterns, anomalies, and structural timeline shifts. By "reducing" 1GB of raw logs into 10KB of meaningful context, the AI agent can analyze the incident incredibly fast and for pennies instead of hundreds of dollars per run.
