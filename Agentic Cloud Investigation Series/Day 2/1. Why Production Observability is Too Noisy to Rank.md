## 1. Why Production Observability is "Too Noisy to Rank"
In a microservices cluster (like the Amazon ECS diagram you showed earlier or a Kubernetes cluster), thousands of events happen every second. When a system crash or an outage occurs, your observability tools (Datadog, Prometheus, CloudWatch) spit out **millions of logs, metrics, and trace links**.
- **The Problem:** Because everything is interconnected, a failure in _Service A_ will cause cascading errors in _Service B, C, and D_.
- **"Too Noisy to Rank"** means that traditional monitoring systems cannot easily tell you which error is the _root cause_ and which ones are just _symptoms_. Sorting through this noise manually takes a massive amount of engineering time.
