## 5. The Anatomy of an Overnight Stream (The Noise)
The text provides a realistic breakdown of how a single root cause creates an overwhelming amount of noise within just two minutes:
- **02:14 AM:** A single security change happens on a production role. (This is the **Root Cause**).
- **Same minute:** 180 `AccessDenied` lines flood your security audit logs because services suddenly lose their access tokens.
- **Seconds later:** Three separate automated monitoring tools send near-identical alerts because their applications are failing.
- **02:16 AM:** Just two minutes later, the human engineer on-call wakes up to a barrage of notifications and has 5 different dashboards open, completely overwhelmed.
