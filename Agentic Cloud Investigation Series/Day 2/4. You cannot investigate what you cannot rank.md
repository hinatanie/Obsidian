## 4. "You cannot investigate what you cannot rank"
When an outage happens, you are hit with a massive wall of text and notifications. If you have 50 different alerts screaming at you simultaneously, you cannot physically look at all of them at once.
- **Ranking** means determining **priority and causality**.
- If you cannot instantly rank which alert is the _cause_ (e.g., an IAM permission change) versus which alerts are just _symptoms_ (e.g., 180 services failing to connect because of that permission change), you will waste precious hours investigating the wrong things.
