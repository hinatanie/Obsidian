## 1.6.1.1.3.1 How RabbitMQ Works (The Mailbox Analogy)
RabbitMQ uses an advanced routing mechanism that is highly flexible. It consists of three primary components:
- Producer (The Sender): The application that creates and sends a message (e.g., a checkout service sending an "Order Placed" notification).
- Exchange (The Post Office): The component that receives messages from producers and decides exactly where to route them based on predefined rules (routing keys).
- Queue (The Mailbox): A buffer that stores the messages until they are consumed by the receiving application.
- Consumer (The Receiver): The application waiting to get the message and process it (e.g., an inventory service or email notification service).
