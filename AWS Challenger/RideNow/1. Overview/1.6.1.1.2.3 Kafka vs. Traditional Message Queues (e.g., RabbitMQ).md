## Kafka vs. Traditional Message Queues (e.g., RabbitMQ)

|Feature|Traditional Message Queues|Apache Kafka|
|---|---|---|
|Data Retention|Messages are deleted immediately after a consumer reads them.|Messages are persisted to a log and stored for a set period (e.g., 7 days), allowing them to be re-read if needed.|
|Scale & Speed|Built for moderate data sizes with complex routing.|Built to handle millions of messages per second with ultra-low millisecond latency.|
|Data Flow|The queue pushes data to consumers.|Consumers pull data from Kafka at their own pace, preventing them from being overwhelmed.|
